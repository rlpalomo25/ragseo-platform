## The framing (fixed)
- **Eyebrow:** YOUR BUYING CHECKLIST
- **Heading:** Before You Buy Any Gutter Guard, **Ask This**
- **Intro:** Flat guards become debris shelves. Hood-style guards let water overshoot. Don't ask "Does it work?" — every company says yes. Ask **how** it solves the failure points that matter.
- **Close:** MasterShield® with AEGIS 5X® can answer every one — because every feature has a job.

## The six canonical questions (do not reword without Karen)
Each maps to an AEGIS 5X job. A guard that can answer all six has been engineered for every failure mode; one that can't, hasn't. Competitors answer some, never all — that's the differentiation.

1. **How does debris keep moving instead of settling?** → *SelfClean Mesh (debris shedding)*
2. **How does heavy rain get into the gutter?** → *HydroVortex (water capture)*
3. **How does the surface stay open over time?** → *organic resistance / SelfClean*
4. **How does it help resist moss, algae, lichen, and bio-growth?** → *CopperCare*
5. **How does the installation protect vulnerable roof-edge shingles?** → *ShingleSafe*
6. **What materials are doing the work — and why?** → *PitchPerfect / materials engineering*

## The page-specific question rule (per page)
On every **BOFU/MOFU** page, the execution plan adds **at least one page-specific question** tied to that page's topic, in the same "ask HOW, make them prove it" framing, worded so MasterShield's answer is the benchmark competitors fail. Examples:
- **Complaints / reviews page:** "Ask them: what's your actual warranty *claim* rate?" (MasterShield: 0.00001%)
- **Cost page:** "Ask them: what's the lifetime cost, not just today's price?"
- **Installation page:** "Ask them: how do you protect the roof-edge shingles during the install?"

## Per-brand rendering (cross-domain duplicate-content rule)
The six questions are AEGIS 5X's jobs, and **both MasterShield and Klean Gutter run on AEGIS 5X**, so the underlying checklist is the same for both brands. But **MasterShield.com and KleanGutter.com are separate domains** — publishing the *identical* block on both is cross-domain duplicate content that dilutes both pages (Doc 163 Per-Brand rule). So:
- The **six questions (the jobs) are canonical and shared** — same substance, same order, on every brand.
- Each brand **renders them in its own words and its own close.** MasterShield: premium-builder / simple-science voice, close "MasterShield® with AEGIS 5X® can answer every one." Klean Gutter: Helpful-Neighbor voice, close names **Klean Gutter.** The intro framing is re-worded per brand too.
- **Never paste one brand's exact block onto the other's domain.** Same logic, different text.
- **MMGG (B2B):** reframe as dealer enablement — "arm your customers with these questions" — and it may name both consumer brands (Doc 163 MMGG rule).
