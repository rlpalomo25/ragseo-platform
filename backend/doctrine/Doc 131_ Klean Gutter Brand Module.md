## 2. Brand Voice & Argument Architecture

The voice and argument structure defined here directly implements the **Brand Argument Architecture (Doc 135)**.

### 2.1 The 7 Layers of Brand Argument

| Layer | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Doctrine** | Homeowners deserve a high-performance, engineered gutter guard without paying the absolute top-tier premium. |
| **Narrative Lens** | The Helpful Neighbor. The narrative focuses on practical value, reliable performance, and smart engineering. It speaks to homeowners who want a serious product that works, without the fluff. |
| **Argument Posture** | Problem → Smart Engineering (AEGIS 5X) → Reliable Performance → Exceptional Value |
| **Claim Hierarchy** | 1. Unsolved Homeowner Pain, 2. Why "Contractor Grade" Fails, 3. The AEGIS 5X Advantage, 4. Long-Term Value |
| **Evidence Profile** | Practical comparisons, long-term durability, engineering logic explained simply, value-over-time metrics |
| **Voice & Tone** | Helpful, practical, straightforward, and confident. Like a knowledgeable neighbor recommending a reliable brand (e.g., Honda, Patagonia). |
| **Reader Cognitive Role** | Smart shopper who wants premium performance and is willing to invest in quality, but demands strong value for their money. |

**Reading Level:** 7th grade — clear, accessible, and free of unnecessary jargon.

### 2.2 Article Archetype Selection

The Router determines which archetype to use based on three factors:

1. **SERP Format Alignment:** If the dominant SERP format is a list, video, or non-narrative, the Router selects an archetype that matches (e.g., Comparison, Buyer Guide).
2. **Keyword Intent & Decision Axis:** The keyword's primary intent and the cluster's Decision Axis are matched to the most appropriate archetype.
3. **Rotation Check:** The Router ensures the selected archetype wasn't used in the immediately preceding article within the same cluster. If it was, the next-best fit is selected.

#### Rotation Protocol

To prevent structural repetition, the system rotates through **12 approved article archetypes**. The rule is simple:

> **Do not repeat the same archetype in consecutive articles within the same cluster.**

#### Klean Gutter Preferred Archetypes

These archetypes align best with the Klean Gutter voice and audience:

- Buyer Guide
- Comparison
- Explainer
- Scenario Narrative

#### All 12 Approved Archetypes

| # | Archetype | Reader Role | Structure |
|---|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Explainer** | Learner | Concept → Why It Matters → How It Works → Key Factors → Implications |
| 2 | **Diagnostic** | Investigator | Symptom → Possible Causes → How to Identify → Explanation → Solutions |
| 3 | **Myth-Buster** | Skeptic | Common Belief → Why Believed → Evidence Against → Correct Explanation → Better Approach |
| 4 | **Comparison** | Evaluator | Decision Problem → Criteria → Side-by-Side → Tradeoffs → Recommendation |

## 7. System Integration & Enforcement

This section defines how the Brand Module connects to and is enforced by other system components. These rules are **mandatory**.

### 7.1 Execution Contract Requirements

When creating an Execution Contract for a Klean Gutter page, the following fields must be explicitly declared:

- **`archetype`**: The selected article archetype from Section 2.2
- **`decision_axis`**: The core user decision the page is built to address, as defined in the **Cluster Architecture Map (Doc 222)**

### 7.2 QA & Enforcement Hooks

The Pre-Publish QA Checklist (Doc 1710) must include checks to verify:

- ✅ **Archetype Rotation:** The selected archetype hasn't been used in the previous article within the same cluster
- ✅ **Argument Structure:** The article contains the full `Problem → Smart Engineering → Reliable Performance → Exceptional Value` argument chain
- ✅ **Tone Compliance:** The voice and tone align with the rules in Section 2.1
- ✅ **Decision Axis Alignment:** The article's content and structure directly support the declared `decision_axis`
- ✅ **Narrative Lens Adherence:** The article consistently frames the narrative through the helpful neighbor lens, focusing on value and performance
- ✅ **Brand Separation:** The article does not mention MasterShield or position Klean Gutter relative to it

### 7.3 Module Dependencies & Hierarchy

- **Required Dependencies:** The following modules are mandatory for any page written for the Klean Gutter brand:
  - **AEGIS 5X Mechanism Authority (Doc 142)**
  - **Trust Layer System (Doc 141)**
  - **Conversion Architecture Module (Doc 140)**

- **Hierarchy Rule:** This Brand Module's voice and tone definitions (Section 2.1) supersede any general guidance in the **Brand Voice Guide (Doc 134)**.

### 7.4 Voice Adaptation for Conversion

While the primary voice is "helpful and practical," the tone must adapt based on the reader's position in the funnel:

| Funnel Stage | Voice Adaptation |
|--------------|-------------------|
| **TOFU / MOFU** (Pillar/Cluster Pages) | Maintain the straightforward, value-driven, 7th-grade reading level |
| **BOFU** (Local Pages & CTA Sections) | Voice becomes more direct, clear, and action-oriented. Shifts from *explaining value* to *presenting the clear solution*. Goal: confident, low-friction path to resolving their long-term problem. |
